I am an undergraduate student in Microelectronics at the South China University of Technology, advised by [Prof. Simei Yang](https://www2.scut.edu.cn/microelectronics/2020/0910/c25586a578837/page.htm). Since August 2026 I have been an exchange student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and a research intern in [Prof. Yuan Xie](https://fact-lab.hkust.edu.hk/)'s FACT Lab.
My research interest is hardware-software co-design for AI and agent workloads: sparse-matrix and GCN accelerators, shared-cache and memory-hierarchy management, and lightweight runtime mechanisms that connect workload behavior to architectural decisions. I care about where a system actually stalls, and about mechanisms whose cost fits the decision they inform.
